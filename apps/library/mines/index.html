How to play

Some squares of the field hide mines. Open every square that doesn't.

  • Tap a square to open it. Your first tap is always safe and always opens an area.
  • A number tells how many of the eight squares around it hide a mine. A square with none around opens its neighbours by itself.
  • Long-press a square (or right-click it) to place a flag where you're sure a mine is. Prefer tapping? Switch the tools below the field from Reveal to Flag.
  • Tap a number that already has all its flags around it to open the rest of its neighbours at once.
  • You win when every safe square is open. Opening a mine ends the field.
  • Stuck? Hint shows a square that is certain from what's open, or tells you when only a guess is left.
