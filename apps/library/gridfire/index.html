How to play

Two thumbs

  • Put a thumb down anywhere on the left half and drag to fly.
  • Drag on the right half to aim. Your ship fires for as long as that thumb is down.
  • Tap the round button to send out a nova that sweeps away every enemy it reaches. Its ring fills as you destroy enemies, and you can hold three.
  • Turn on one-thumb mode in Settings if you would rather let the ship aim for you.
  • On a keyboard: WASD to move, arrow keys or the mouse to aim and fire, Space for a nova, P to pause.

Scoring

Destroyed enemies leave glowing sparks. Fly through them to warm the meter under your score. Each time it fills, your multiplier rises a step, up to ×8.

The meter cools while enemies are about, and when it runs dry the multiplier drops a step. A higher multiplier widens your fire, from two streams to a five-way spread.

Your hull takes four hits. Each hit costs a segment and two steps of multiplier, and every wave you clear mends one segment.

Power-ups

Every wave sends in at least one elite, ringed in gold. Elites take a few extra hits and are worth triple points, and each one drops a capsule. Fly through it to take the power-up. Its timer runs around the icon under the pause button.

    Upgrades

    After waves 1, 3 and 5, and after every second wave in Endless, the action stops and you choose one of three upgrades. They stack until the sector ends.

      Synergies

      Two power-ups that work well together become a synergy while both are active. Capsules often bring a partner for a power-up you hold.

        Weapons and ships

        Choose them in the hangar before a run. Each weapon plays differently, and power-ups and upgrades work with all of them.

          Pilot level and talents

          Every run earns experience, even one that ends early: for your score, each wave cleared and each sector cleared. Each level brings a talent point, and some levels open a new ship, weapon or starting option. As you grow stronger, the waves grow a little larger.

          Field guide

          Most of them keep time with the music's beat, even with the music off. Watch the rhythm and you will see the next move coming.
