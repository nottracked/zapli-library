How to play

Two thumbs

  • Put a thumb down anywhere on the left half and drag to fly.
  • Drag on the right half to aim. Your ship fires for as long as that thumb is down.
  • Tap the star button to fire a bomb that clears every enemy around you.
  • Turn on one-thumb mode in Settings if you would rather let the ship aim for you.
  • On a keyboard: WASD to move, arrow keys or the mouse to aim and fire, Space for a bomb, P to pause.

Scoring

Destroyed enemies drop glowing shards. Fly through them to fill the meter under your score and raise the multiplier, up to ×10.

A higher multiplier widens your fire, from two parallel streams to a five-way spread. Losing a ship resets it to ×1.

Extra ships arrive every 40,000 points and extra bombs every 60,000.

Power-ups

Every wave sends in at least one elite, ringed in gold. Elites take a few extra hits and are worth triple points, and each one drops a capsule. Fly through it to take the power-up. Its timer runs around the icon under the pause button.

    Upgrades

    After waves 1, 3 and 5, and after every second wave in Endless, the action stops and you choose one of three upgrades. They stack until the sector ends.
